Oil & gas royalty administration

  • U.S. Energy Information Administration — Today in Energy, March 31, 2026

    The federal source for American crude oil production, including the 2025 record of 13.6 million barrels a day and the Permian Basin's share of it.

  • U.S. Government Accountability Office — GAO-26-107669, November 2025

    The audit of federal oil and gas royalty payments, covering more than $14 billion in fiscal 2024 revenue and the adjustments that reached back years.

  • U.S. Department of the Interior — $16.45 Billion in Fiscal Year 2024 Energy Revenue, November 8, 2024

    The announcement of the Office of Natural Resources Revenue's fiscal 2024 disbursements, including the split between states, Tribes, and federal funds.

  • Chesapeake Exploration v. Morton Production Co. — Wyoming Supreme Court, 2025

    The 2025 decision affirming a Royalty Payment Act violation where disputed production proceeds were withheld without being placed in escrow.

  • Wyoming Supreme Court — Royalty Payment Act decisions

    The court's published royalty-payment rulings, including the line of cases that defines when operators must escrow disputed proceeds.

Music royalty platforms

  • IFPI — Global Music Report 2025, March 19, 2025

    The industry's annual account of recorded music revenue, including the $29.6 billion total for 2024 and streaming's $20.4 billion share.

    The United States recorded music figures for 2024, including $17.7 billion at retail and the first year with 100 million paid subscriptions.

    The performance rights organization's record year, with $1.835 billion in revenue and $1.696 billion available for royalty distributions.

  • SoundExchange — $12 Billion Cumulative Distribution Milestone, February 24, 2025

    The digital performance royalty collector's account of passing $12 billion in cumulative distributions since 2003.

    The MLC's yearly summary of mechanical royalty distributions from streaming services, including match rates and royalties still pending.

  • Music Business Worldwide — The MLC Passes $4.2 Billion Distributed, September 29, 2026

    The report on the MLC's cumulative payouts and its plan to begin market-share distributions of unmatched and unclaimed royalties in April 2027.
