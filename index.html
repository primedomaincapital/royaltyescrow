Royalty escrow is the one escrow name where the exact phrase is already a named, sold service in more than one paying industry. Wilmington Trust — one of the largest institutional trust companies in the United States — lists royalty escrow as a named service on its business escrow page. It is not a coined category. It is a line item a trust bank already sells.

The oil and gas side is the more forceful case, because the law compels it. Under Wyoming's Royalty Payment Act, an operator that withholds production proceeds when ownership is disputed must place the money in escrow — and an operator that fails to pay on time owes the unpaid amount plus 18 percent annual interest. In 2025 the Wyoming Supreme Court affirmed a violation in Chesapeake Exploration v. Morton Production Co., where disputed proceeds were withheld without being placed in escrow as the statute requires. The court has said the statute's purpose plainly: to stop oil producers from retaining other people's money for their own use.

The sums in motion are large enough to support a specialist. Federal oil and gas royalties alone generated more than $14 billion in revenue in fiscal 2024, and companies paid roughly $96 billion in royalties over fiscal 2014–2024, according to the U.S. Government Accountability Office. The Office of Natural Resources Revenue disbursed $16.45 billion in fiscal 2024 — the fourth-largest annual disbursement since 1982. On the music side, global recorded music revenue reached $29.6 billion in 2024, and Spotify alone paid $10 billion to the music industry that year. Every one of those dollars passes through somebody's hands before it reaches its owner.

Exact, and built for a reachable buyer pool. The buyers are nameable: the royalty administrators who track mineral interests, the trust banks whose escrow desks already sell this service, the music royalty platforms that hold unmatched payouts, and the oil and gas operators who must escrow disputed proceeds by law. They all use the same two words. This domain is those two words, in order, with nothing added.

Q.02

What is royalty escrow?

Royalty escrow is an arrangement in which royalty payments are held by a neutral third party until the rightful recipient is established. In Wyoming, the Royalty Payment Act requires operators to place disputed production proceeds in escrow, and charges 18 percent annual interest when they do not.
